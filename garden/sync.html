What is included?

Your garden checklists, workout log, recovery notes, garden settings, and any notes saved in the combined garden. The dedicated Skincare Studio uses different browser storage and is not included in this cloud backup. Export its records separately from Skincare Studio.

Only the signed-in owner can read or write their cloud backup through the app. The database uses row-level security. The app does not share its contents with ChatGPT automatically.
